## 4. Electives

CMO 25 sample (pp. 18, 20-21): four elective slots of 3 units each, 12 units in total: PT101 Platform Technologies (2/1), IPT101 Integrative Programming and Technologies 1 (2/2; also a core course in the p. 17-18 list), PF102 Event-Driven Programming (3/1), SIA102 Systems Integration and Architecture 2 (4/2). Recommended electives listed on p. 18: IPT102, PT101, WS101, PF101, SIA102, HCI102 (3 units each; HCI102 blank).

BCP dataset: ITE1 IT ELECTIVE 1 (Web Fundamental) 3 u 2/1; ITE2 IT ELECTIVE 2 (Advance Javaor OOP) 3 u 2/2; ITE3 IT ELECTIVE 3 (Research) 3 u 3/1; ITE4 IT ELECTIVE 4 (System Integration and Architecture 2) **5 u** 4/1. Total 14 units. The only elective whose title matches a CMO course is ITE4 (SIA102, which is **3 units** in 4th year 2nd semester in the CMO); the BCP value is 5 units in 4th year 1st semester. The 14 comes from 3+3+3+5 in the team dataset (manifest and database agree).


## 5. The "Curriculum Map for the BSIT" (Art. V Sec. 10)

- **Where:** printed pp. 28 to 34 (it follows the BSCS and BSIS maps; the BSIT map begins below the tail of the previous table on p. 28 and ends with PRAC101 on p. 34, before Sec. 11). Columns IT01 to IT13 with I (introduced), E (enabled) or D (demonstrated) marks per course learning outcome (LO1, LO2, ...), plus a pre-requisite and a units column.
- **Courses listed (29):** CC101, CC102, CC103, CC104, CC105, CC106, MS102, PF101 (Object-Oriented Programming), PF102 (Event-Driven Programming), HCI101, HCI102, IM101 (Advanced Database Systems), PT101, NET101, NET102, IPT101, IPT102, SIA101, SIA102, SP101, SP102, IAS101, IAS102, WS101, WS102, CAP101, CAP102, SA101, PRAC101. (MS101 is the pre-requisite of MS102 and is in the course lists but I did not see its own map row.)
- **Match with BSIT 2026 by code:** 19 of the 29 map courses have a subject with the same code in BSIT 2026 (CC101, CC102 as CCS1102/CC102, CC103, CC104, CC105, CC106, MS102, HCI101, IM101, NET101, NET102, IPT101, SIA101, SP101, IAS101, IAS102, CAP101, CAP102, SA101). The other 10 are not placed: PF101, PF102, HCI102, PT101, IPT102, SIA102, SP102, WS101, WS102, PRAC101. Titles differ for MS102, IM101, SIA101, CAP101, SA101 and CAP102 (the last reads "CAPSTONE 012"). I did not match by title where the code is missing.
- **Not imported.** The marks describe the CMO sample courses, not BCP courses, and a link in this system needs a rationale written for BCP's own course.
